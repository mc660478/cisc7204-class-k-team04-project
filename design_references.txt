DESIGN DECISIONS
A white canvas, IBM Plex type, direct series labels, horizontal years and quiet grid
lines keep the observations central. Purple identifies prices; teal, squares and a
dashed line distinguish earnings. Growth uses teal bars with a visible white hatch.
Small teal marks identify zero growth. Annual points and straight segments remain.
Line axes start at 80 (rebased trends) and 60 (relative pressure), explicitly disclosed
beneath each chart; bar charts retain zero. The 2019–2021 price plateau is annotated
without shading that could imply uncertainty. No smoothing or secondary axis is used.
All 12 observed earlier base years remain in the sensitivity chart.

Both standard (1200×760) and report (1200×620) layouts are exported at 2× PNG resolution,
plus vector PDF/SVG. Report footers are 20 px and axis labels 24 px at the source size.
All seven bundled font faces are embedded into each SVG and interactive HTML. PDF
font embedding is checked separately. HTML includes Plotly inline, no MathJax, and an
accessible numerical table. The notebook reader uses saved static outputs and no JS.

This redesign changes presentation only. Source tables, calculations, statistics and
scientific interpretation remain those of the completed final analysis.
