EXPLANATORY NOTES 
 
 
1. Methodology 
The Employment Survey is a monthly household sample survey, with the reference period referring to the 7 days 
before enumeration. Samples are selected after exclusion of collective living quarters, such as military camps, hospitals, 
prisons, dormitories and care homes for the elderly. Total sample size for the whole year of 2025 consists of 16,800 
residential units and the individuals living in these units are covered in the survey. Therefore, Macao SAR residents and 
non-resident workers who work in the Macao SAR but live outside the territory (e.g. Zhuhai, Hong Kong Special 
Administrative Region) are excluded from the survey; information on these individuals (number, age group, etc.) is 
extrapolated according to the movement records. 
Results of this report are extrapolated based on the information obtained from the samples that are subject to 
sampling errors. To illustrate the precision of the estimates of different variables, the respective standard deviation and 
coefficient of variation are listed in the table below.

Response rate is the ratio of the number of residential units successfully interviewed to the number of occupied units 
in the sample. In 2025, a total of 15,855 units were occupied, comprising 13,395 successful interviews, 181 refusals and 
2,279 non-contacts; therefore, the response rate stood at 84.5%. 
 
2. Glossary 
Economically active population / Labour force 
Individuals aged 16 and above who are available to participate in the production of goods and/or services during the 7 
days before enumeration. It comprises the employed and unemployed. 
Educational attainment 
The highest level of education an individual successfully completed. Education is classified into primary education, 
junior secondary education, senior secondary education, tertiary education and others. "Primary education" refers to 
completion of grade six; those who fail to complete grade six are classified as "others".
