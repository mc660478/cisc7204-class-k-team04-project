 In 2025, the overall average residential property price index decreased by 9. 4% year-on-year, and the indices for existing residential units and pre -
sale residential units fell by 9.3% and 16.4% respectively.
 For October - December 2025, the overall residential property price index increased by 0.1% from the previous period (September - November
2025); the index for Taipa & Coloane rose by 1.0% while that for the Macao Peninsula dropped by 0.3%.
 The index for existing residential units grew by 0.2% from the previous period; the index for Taipa & Coloane climbed by 1.1% while that for the
Macao Peninsula went down by 0.1%. Analysed by age of building, the index for those between 6 and 10 years old rose by 3.6%, whereas the
index for those over 20 years old decreased by 1.9%. Meanwhile, the index for pre-sale residential units went up by 0.7%.
• With respect to usable floor area, the index for residential units
with a usable floor area of 100 square metres and over for
October - December 2025 went up by 1.6% from the previous
period, while the index for those with a floor area between 75
and 99.9 square metres decreased by 2.0%.
• In terms of high- and low-rise buildings, the index for residential
units of buildings with seven storeys or less dropped by 0.2%,
whereas the index for those with more than seven storeys rose
by 0.4%.

• In the fourth quarter of 2025, the overall residential property price
index increased by 0.1% from the third quarter. The index for pre-
sale residential units rose by 1.5%, while that for existing residential

• The index for existing residential units in the Macao Peninsula went
up by 0.4% quarter-on-quarter; the indices for those in the Parish of
