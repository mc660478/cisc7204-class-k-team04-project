Unemployed population (Unemployed residents) 
Refers to individuals aged 16 and above who fulfil the following conditions: 
1) have not had a job during the 7 days before enumeration; 
2) have been available for work during the 7 days before enumeration; and 
3) have sought work for the past 30 days. 
The unemployed comprise individuals searching for their first job and those searching for a new job. 
Unemployment rate 
Percentage share of the number of the unemployed to the labour force. 
Working hours 
Refer to the number of hours actually worked during the 7 days before enumeration, which include hours of paid and 
unpaid overtime work but exclude meal breaks. If the individual is absent from work due to public holidays, annual leave, 
sickness or other reasons, his or her working hours will be calculated based on normal hours of work.

Rounding of figures 
Numbers of persons are rounded to the nearest hundred. Percentage changes and percentage shares are calculated 
from unrounded figures and rounded to one decimal place. 
Due to rounding, some totals may not correspond with the sum of the separate figures. 
 
Employment earnings and hours of work 
In the Employment Survey report, employment earnings are expressed in Macao Patacas. Unless otherwise specified, 
"employment earnings" refers to the total monthly income from all jobs; "hours of work" is the number of hours worked per 
week in all jobs. In the statistical tables, "industry" and "occupation" are classified according to the main job of the 
respondents. 
 
Please refer to the Employment Survey Annex for details on the methodology of the Survey.
