EXPLANATORY NOTES 
 
 
 
1. Methodology 
The Employment Survey is conducted on a monthly basis, with the reference period referring to the 
7 days immediately before the day of interview. Samples are selected after exclusion of collective living 
quarters, such as military camp, hospi tal, prison, dormitory, elderly home,  etc. Total sample size for the 
whole year of 2015 consists of 16 800 residential units  and the observation unit is individuals living in 
these units. Therefore, survey results do not cover Macao resident s and non-resident workers who work 
in Macao but live in Zhuhai or the neighbouring ar eas; information on these individuals (number, age 
group, etc.) is extrapolated according to the records from the Immigration Department. 
 
Results of this report are extrapolated based on the information obtained from the samples that are 
subject to sampling errors. To illustrate the precis ion of the estimates of different variables, the 
respective standard error and coefficient of variation are listed in the table below.
