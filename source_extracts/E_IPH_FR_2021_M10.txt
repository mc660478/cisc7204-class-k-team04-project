 The overall residential property price index increased by 0.3% over the previous period (September - November 2021); the indices for the Macao
Peninsula and Taipa & Coloane rose by 0.3% and 0.5% respectively.
 The index for existing residential units decreased by 0.2% from the previ ous period, with the indices for those in the Macao Peninsula and Taipa &
Coloane dropping by 0.1% and 0.5% respectively. Analysed by year of buildin g completion, the indices for residential units of buildings between 6
and 10 years old and for those between 11 and 20 years old dipped by 2.8% an d 1.5% respectively. Meanwhile, the index for pre-sale residential
units increased by 2.8%.
 In 2021, the overall average residential property price index decreased by 0.1% year-on-year.
• The indices for residential uni ts with a usable floor area of 100
square metres or more and for those with a floor area between
50 and 74.9 square metres fell by 1.5% and 0.2% respectively.
Meanwhile, the indices for residential units with a floor area of
less than 50 square metres and for those with a floor area
between 75 and 99.9 square metres both went up by 0.7%.
• In terms of high- and low-rise buildings, the index for residential
units of buildings with seven storeys or less rose by 1.4%,
whereas the index for those with more than seven storeys
dropped by 0.4%.

São Lourenço• The overall residential property price index in the fourth quarter of
2021 decreased by 0.6% from the third quarter. The index for
existing residential units fell by 1.4%, while that for pre-sale
residential units grew by 7.2%.
• The index for existing residential units in the Macao Peninsula went
down by 1.8% quarter-to-quarter; the indices for those in the Parish
of São Lázaro and the Parish of Santo António dipped by 9.9% and
2.0% respectively, whereas both the indices for those in the Parish of
São Lourenço and the Parish of Sé increased by 0.8%.
• When compared with the fourth quarter of 2020, the overall
residential property price index and the index for existing residential
units in the fourth quarter of 2021 decreased by 0.4% and 0.2%
respectively, whereas the index for pre-sale residential units
increased by 8.9%.
