Employed population 
Individuals aged 16 and above who work for pay or profit for at least 1 hour during the 7 days before enumeration. It 
also includes unpaid family workers, employees who are absent from work but have job attachment to the employer, as well 
as employers or the self-employed who are on leave. 
Employed population comprises employed residents and non-resident workers living in the Macao SAR. 
Employed residents 
Local population aged 16 and above who work for pay or profit for at least 1 hour during the 7 days before 
enumeration. It also includes unpaid family workers, employees who are absent from work but have job attachment to the 
employer, as well as employers or the self-employed who are on leave. Non-resident workers are excluded. 
Employed residents who changed jobs 
Full-time employed residents who have changed their job (including changes of industry engaged, company / 
organisation, employer or status in employment) during the 12 months prior to enumeration. 
Employment earnings 
Refer to gross earnings from all jobs in the month prior to the enumeration. For employees, it refers to remuneration 
received, including salaries, commissions, gratuities, overtime pay, bonuses, allowances, double pay, rewards, etc. For 
employers and the self-employed, it refers to profits earned from running their business, i.e. operating receipts less 
production costs and depreciation of assets. 
Hours actually worked 
Refer to the number of hours actually worked during the 7 days before enumeration, which include hours of paid and 
unpaid overtime work, excluding meal breaks and absence from work due to public holidays, annual leave, sickness or other 
reasons. 
Labour force participation rate 
The proportion of the economically active population to the population aged 16 and above. 
Resident labour force 
Local population aged 16 and above who are available to participate in the production of goods and/or services during 
the 7 days before enumeration. Non-resident workers are excluded. 
Underemployed population 
Refers to employed persons, regardless of their status in employment, who work involuntarily for less than 35 hours 
during the 7 days before enumeration and who meet any of the following conditions: 
1) are available to take on additional work; or 
2) are looking for extra work during the 30 days before enumeration. 
Underemployed residents 
Refer to employed residents, regardless of their status in employment, who work involuntarily for less than 35 hours 
during the 7 days before enumeration and who meet any of the following conditions: 
1) are available to take on additional work; or 
2) are looking for extra work during the 30 days before enumeration. 
Underemployment rate 
Percentage share of the number of the underemployed to the labour force.
