 The overall residential property price index increased by 0.2% over the previous period (September - November 2020); the index for the Macao
Peninsula rose by 0.3%, while that for Taipa & Coloane fell by 0.3%.
 The index for existing residential units went up by 0.1%, with the index for those in the Macao Peninsula rising by 0.3% while the index for those in
Taipa & Coloane dropping by 0.3%. Analysed by year of building completi on, the indices for residential units built between 6 and 10 years ago and
for those completed in 5 years or less increased by 3.8% and 1.3% respec tively, whereas the index for residential units built between 11 and 20
years ago dipped by 2.0%. Meanwhile, the index for pre-sale residential units grew by 0.1%.
 In 2020, the overall residential property price index rose by 0.1% year-on-year. The index for pre-sale residential units went up by 1.5%, while that
for existing residential units dropped by 0.1%.
• The indices for residential units with a usable floor area between
75 and 99.9 square metres and for those with a floor area of 100
square metres or more decreased by 1.4% and 0.9% respectively,
while the index for those with a floor area between 50 and 74.9
square metres rose by 0.1%.
• In terms of high- and low-rise build ings, the index for residential
units of buildings with seven storeys or less dropped by 0.5%,
whereas the index for those with more than seven storeys
increased by 0.1%.
